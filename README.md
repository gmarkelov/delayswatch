# delayswatch
Observing and predicting delays on IrishRail sevice.

Not long ago I became a frequent customer of IrishRail and was quick to notice the issues with the frequent delays of the service and keeping thier customers updated on the delays. Such unawareness leads to customers not arriving to their destionation on time that they have planned, and may lead to missing their next transport of choice as usually train stations serve a transfer hub to buses or trams.

To tackle this issue I decided to dig deep into the data that is provided by IrishRail(https://api.irishrail.ie/realtime/) and DATA.GOV.IE(NTA GTFS), and see if it is possible to track the real delay times in real-time, and/or predict the possible delays utilizing Machine Learning methods.
